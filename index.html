✦ What Makes openrig Special?

Think of openrig as the control center for your AI agents. Instead of running one AI assistant at a time, you can create an entire team of working agents. Each one gets a specific role, like "researcher," "code reviewer," or "designer." They share a workspace and keep track of what needs to be done.

🔁 Works with Three Main AI Tools

openrig connects to Claude Code, Codex from OpenAI, and Pi. Choose your favorite—or mix them—to get maximum creativity and brainpower.

💬 Persistent Teams & Roles

Give each agent a clear job. Set roles like "Data Analyst" or "Code Reviewer." The team remembers context, so they don't need to be restarted from scratch for every new task.

📦 Owned Work & Shared Context

Agents can pick up files, modify code, and keep track of progress. Everything stays organized, so nothing gets lost when switching between agents.

🧑‍💻 About the Project

openrig is designed for everyone who wants to get more out of AI tools. Behind the scenes, it uses modern technologies like TypeScript and a strong command-line interface (CLI) combined with a beautiful terminal interface powered by tmux for handling multiple windows. But you don't need to know any of that to use it!

The goal is simple: take the power of AI agents and organize them in a way that feels like a professional team, with everyone working together on your computer.

Why choose openrig?

  • Full control over your agents—no cloud dependency.
  • Multi-agent systems in one process.
  • Completely free and open source.
  • Works on any typical Windows setup.
