vit2snn: layer-wise distillation of a Vision Transformer into a spiking ViT

Converts a pretrained ViT-Tiny/16 into a spiking Vision Transformer (4 time steps) in two phases: the student first copies the teacher layer by layer, then is fine-tuned on the labels with a distillation loss. Trained and evaluated on Imagenette on a laptop (Apple M2), with firing-rate profiling, an analytical energy estimate, diagnostics and a three-seed ablation study.

Layer-wise distillation adds +13.0 and +10.3 top-1 points over fine-tuning alone (5 and 12 epochs, positive in all 3 seeds). Best student: 70.2% top-1 on the 10 Imagenette classes with ~7.5% of neurons firing, at an estimated 0.56 mJ per image vs 5.77 mJ for the teacher.

Speech is transcribed with Whisper; DeBERTa detects intent and emotion, BERT extracts named entities, BlenderBot generates candidate replies that a cross-encoder reranks, and the answer is translated to French and spoken aloud. Navigating water currents with Markov decision processes

Modelled navigation through obstacles and water currents (e.g. a kayak in rapids) as a Markov decision process with stochastic transitions. Solved it exactly with value iteration (Bellman operator), then compared Q-learning and SARSA under ε-greedy exploration and tuned their learning and exploration rates.

Q-learning learns the shortest, riskier path; SARSA learns a safer detour away from obstacles.
